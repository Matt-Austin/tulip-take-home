# Exercise 2F: The field we would not trust

**Health.** It reads like an early-warning flag but behaves like a record of the outcome. Of FY26's 348 closed renewals, those marked **At-Risk churned 0 of 36 times (0%)**, against 94% for Critical (82 of 87) and 2% for Healthy (5 of 225). A real risk scale has a middle grade that churns somewhere in between. This one has a middle grade that never churns and a top grade that almost always does, which is what you get when Critical is set once a customer has decided to leave. Even among renewals whose notes carry risk language, At-Risk churned 0 of 23 while Critical churned 82 of 84. The separation is too clean to be a forecast. It also gives false alarms: 14 FY26 renewals whose CSM notes say the flag was stale were marked At-Risk or Critical, and all 14 renewed. Health is excluded from the FY27 model. That matters because 88 open FY27 renewals are marked At-Risk today, and on FY26 history that label means close to nothing.

The runner-up is the AE's Probability and Forecast Category. On closed renewals they simply record the outcome (every FY26 churn is Omitted at 0%, every renewal Closed at 100%). On open renewals they barely move: 231 of 245 open FY27 renewals at Open Renewal sit at 75% Best Case, including 131 of the 143 whose notes carry risk language.
