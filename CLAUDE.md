## Data rules (traps called out in the dictionary)
- **Fiscal year:** runs Feb 1 to Jan 31 and is named for the January it ends in. FY27 = Feb 2026 to Jan 2027 (the current year). Quarters: Q1 Feb–Apr, Q2 May–Jul, Q3 Aug–Oct, Q4 Nov–Jan.
- **Company Size (Employees):** a label (SMB / SME / MM / ENT), not headcount. There are four values vs three planning tiers. Reconcile SMB vs SME explicitly, or state that it can't be reconciled.
- **FY26 Segment:** an industry vertical rollup, NOT size.
- **Won/Lost:** spans both lifecycles, so renewed renewals read as Won. Never mix renewals into new-business win rate. Handle the 47 hardware/qualification rows and admin stages (GTM Ops Review, 0 With Finance, Rejected, Ordered / Won, Omitted) deliberately.
- **Health, CSM Health Note, Gong Highlight:** populated on new-business rows where they're meaningless. Use them only on renewals. Health lags and is inconsistent across CSMs.
- **Renewal ARR:** appears on some new-business rows. It often disagrees with Total Booking on pure renewals. Decide and document which to use.
- **New Committed ARR:** populated on ~1,030 of 1,458 rows.
- **Weighted:** an AE opinion, not a measurement.
- **Partner fields:** Generated by and Partner of Record disagree in both directions.
- **Age columns:** Age and Opp Age Recal can disagree.
- **Planning assumptions** (SME 10%, MM 35%, ENT 15%): comparison only.
